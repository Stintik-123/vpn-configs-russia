About VPN-Configs-Russia

Why we built this, and what makes us different

The Problem We Solve

Users searching for VPN configs face a constant challenge:

  • ❌ Thousands of configs to choose from
  • ❌ Most are outdated or non-functional
  • ❌ No way to verify quality automatically
  • ❌ Manual testing wastes hours

Our Solution

VPN-Configs-Russia automates everything:

  • ✅ Automatically collects from sources
  • ✅ Removes duplicates and junk
  • ✅ Tests each config for availability
  • ✅ Publishes only verified configs
  • ✅ Updates every 2 hours

Why It Matters

This is infrastructure, not a VPN service. We don't run servers, manage accounts, or sell subscriptions.

We're a transparency layer. A quality filter. A bridge between users and free configs.

Open Source

Full source code on GitHub. MIT licensed. No hidden functionality.

Anyone can audit, fork, or contribute.
